Tokens

Named values. Switch day and night in the header and they all follow.

Colour

One accent. Everything else is ink and paper.

  • Page--bg
  • Page, end of the gradient--bg-deep
  • Card--surface
  • Card, raised--surface-hi
  • Text--ink
  • Secondary text--ink-lo
  • Hairline--line
  • Accent--accent
  • Accent, tint--accent-soft
color: var(--accent);

Type

M PLUS 1 for words, M PLUS 1 Code for labels and code.

  • Section title--fs-h2
  • Card title--fs-h3
  • Body text--fs-body
  • Secondary text--fs-small
  • Label--fs-label
font-size: var(--fs-h2);

Space and shape

One rhythm, fluid with the screen.

  • Page edge--edge
  • Between sections--section
  • Under a section head--head
  • Corner--radius
padding-inline: var(--edge);

Motion

Out for what appears, move for what travels. Point at the lane.

  • --ease-out
  • --ease-move
transition: translate 600ms var(--ease-out);
