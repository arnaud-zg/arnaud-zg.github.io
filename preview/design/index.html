Tokens

One accent. Everything else is ink and paper.

Type

M PLUS 1 for words, M PLUS 1 Code for labels and code.

  • Section title--fs-h2
  • Card title--fs-h3
  • Body text--fs-body
  • Secondary text--fs-small
  • Label--fs-label
font-size: var(--fs-h2);

Space and shape

One rhythm, fluid with the screen.

  • Page edge--edge
  • Between sections--section
  • Under a section head--head
  • Corner--radius
padding-inline: var(--edge);

Motion

Out for what appears, in for what leaves, move for what travels; each with its duration. Point at the lane.

  • --ease-out · --dur-enter
  • --ease-in · --dur-leave
  • --ease-move · --dur-move
transition: opacity var(--dur-enter) var(--ease-out);
